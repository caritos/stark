## Description
Stark puts the events and reminders you already have in Apple Calendar and Reminders into one fast agenda. Pay once: no subscription, no account, no ads, no tracking.

ONE AGENDA
Events and reminders in one scrolling list, grouped by day. Overdue reminders wait at the top of today. Tick one off, with a moment to undo.

SEE AS FAR AS YOU NEED
Day, week, month, quarter and year views. The year view tints your busy days.

PLAN YOUR DAY
Drag tasks onto a timeline as time blocks next to your events. Resize a block, rework the plan, and your due dates stay as they were.

ADD IT QUICKLY
Type "Lunch with Sarah at 1pm tomorrow" and fill the form in one tap. Save templates for the things you add often. Build repeats such as the first Tuesday of the month.

KEEP IT IN VIEW
- Up Next widget for the Home Screen and Lock Screen
- Calendar sets: switch between work, home or any group you save
- Join button for video calls, and a card for events in other time zones
- Birthdays with ages
- Optional weather forecast, off until you turn it on

APPLE WATCH
See what is coming up, tick off a reminder, or add one by voice or keyboard. Your iPhone does the saving and needs to be in reach.

PRIVATE
Your events and reminders stay in Apple's Calendar and Reminders. Stark has no analytics and no accounts. Access to Calendar and Reminders is required. The only internet request Stark makes is the weather forecast, if you turn it on, using your approximate location.
