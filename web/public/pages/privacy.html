STARK

Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

Stark collects no data about you; the only network connection Stark makes is the optional weather request. Your reminders and events are kept on your device, in your own Apple Calendar and Reminders, which Apple syncs through iCloud if you use it. Weather is the only time Stark makes a network connection: it is optional, off by default, and described under WEATHER. Nothing is ever sent to us. If you use the Apple Watch app, see APPLE WATCH for what moves between your iPhone and your watch.

APPLE CALENDAR AND REMINDERS

Stark keeps your events and to-dos in Apple Calendar and Reminders. It needs full access to both, and before iOS asks, Stark shows a screen that says what the access is for. You can withdraw access in iOS Settings at any time; Stark then shows that screen again and cannot add items.

Stark reads your Apple Calendar events and Apple Reminders on your device and shows them in its agenda, month and year views, Search, the planner and the Weekly Review. Stark saves what you add, edit, complete or delete in Stark to Apple Calendar and Reminders: new events go to the default calendar and new reminders to the default list you choose in Settings > Apple Calendar and Reminders, where you also choose which calendars and lists show. Items on calendars you cannot change, and events you were invited to, are shown but not changed by Stark. If you use iCloud for Calendar or Reminders, Apple syncs these items to your other devices like any other item; that is handled by Apple, not by Stark.

Apple cannot hold everything Stark offers, so a small file of Stark's own on this device keeps the rest: the planner blocks you place for Apple reminders, any attended or skipped marks saved on Apple events by earlier versions, and a record of which of Stark's older items the one-time import copied into Apple. It holds Apple's item identifiers and these plans and marks, not copies of your events or reminders, stays in Stark's private storage and is never sent anywhere by Stark (like Stark's other files, it is part of your device backup).

Stark does not send your calendar or reminder data to us or anyone else unless you choose to share it (the one copy that moves on its own goes to your own Apple Watch, if you use it: see APPLE WATCH): the share button on today's and yesterday's header exports that day's agenda as text, and the share button in Weekly Review exports that week's summary, Apple items included in both, to wherever you send it.

CONTACTS

Contacts access is optional. Events on Apple's Birthdays calendar show a cake, and if you allow it, their age. The first time Stark loads a birthday from a calendar you show, iOS asks whether Stark may read your contacts. If you allow it, Stark reads only the birth year of the people in your Birthdays calendar, on your device, to show "Turns N" under their name. The birth year is used only for that: it is never stored or sent anywhere by Stark, and Stark does not read, copy or keep any other contact detail. If you decline, or withdraw access in iOS Settings, birthday events still show the cake, without the age.

WIDGET

The widget reads your calendar and reminders on your device, the same way the app does, to show what is coming up. It is optional and makes no network connection. To show the same calendars as the app, Stark keeps one small list in an App Group shared with the widget: only the ids of the calendars you hide in Stark, no titles, events or reminders. It stays on your device.

APPLE WATCH

The Apple Watch app is optional. Your iPhone sends the watch a copy of today's and tomorrow's event and reminder titles and times (and overdue reminders) over the system's phone-to-watch link (the system carries it by Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or Apple's relay), and the watch keeps that copy on the watch to show the Up Next list and the complications. The iPhone builds the copy with the same calendars you show in Stark. The watch does not read your calendar or reminders itself and does not ask for access to them.

Completing a reminder or adding an item on the watch sends the request back to your iPhone, which saves it to Apple Reminders or to your default calendar or list. If the iPhone is out of reach, the request waits on the watch and is sent later. When you add an item by dictation, the dictation uses the system's speech recognition, which Apple may process; Stark does not receive the audio. Nothing else leaves your devices, Stark makes no network connection for the watch, and nothing is sent to us.

WEATHER

Weather is optional and off by default. Until you turn it on in Settings, Stark makes no network connection and does not ask for your location. When you turn it on, iOS asks whether Stark may use your location while you use the app. Stark asks for an approximate location only, rounded to about 11 km (0.1 degree), and sends just that position to MET Norway's public forecast service (api.met.no) over https, to show a forecast in the agenda's day headers. Stark sends nothing else: no identifiers, no account, no calendar or reminder data. The position is never stored or logged by Stark, and the forecast is kept only in memory on your device. Turn Weather off and Stark stops asking. MET Norway receives the request like any website would, including your IP address, and handles it under its own terms; we receive nothing. Forecast data from MET Norway.

DATA STORAGE

Items Stark created before it saved to Apple Calendar and Reminders may still be stored in files inside Stark's private storage on your device; earlier versions could copy them into Apple Calendar and Reminders once. Stark no longer reads these old files: they remain on your device, untouched, as a backup, and are deleted only when you delete the app. Stark does not use iCloud for these files and does not sync them between devices.

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple's backup includes Stark's files along with your other apps. That backup is handled entirely by Apple using your Apple ID. We have no access to it and never receive your data.

DATA COLLECTION

Stark does not collect, or send to us or anyone else, any of the following:

— Personal information or identifiers
— Usage data or analytics
— Crash reports or diagnostics
— Reminder or event content or metadata, except the copy your iPhone sends to your own Apple Watch (see APPLE WATCH)

Stark does not ask for access to your photos or notifications. It asks for access to your calendars and reminders, as described above, and optionally to your contacts (under CONTACTS) and your approximate location (under WEATHER), only when you turn those features on or use them. Apart from the optional weather position described above, Stark collects, transmits and stores no location data.

LINKS

If you tap a link in Stark, such as an event's URL or a link in Settings, it opens in your browser. That site's own privacy policy applies to your visit.

THIRD PARTIES

Stark contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries. No data is shared with any third party.

CHILDREN

Stark does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
