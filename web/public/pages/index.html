STARK

Your calendar and reminders, one agenda.

Calendar & Week Planner for iPhone

Stark is a fast calendar and planner for iPhone. It puts the events and reminders you already have in Apple Calendar and Reminders into one scrolling agenda — no account, no subscription.

Some of what follows arrives with version 3.0, which is not yet on the App Store.

See as far as you need

Day, week, month, quarter and year views, plus the agenda with its calendar strip. The year view tints your busy days.

Add things quickly

Type a phrase such as “Lunch with Sarah at 1pm tomorrow” and fill the form in one tap. Save the things you add often as templates. Repeat anything on your own rule.

Keep it in view

An Up Next widget for the Home Screen and Lock Screen. Saved calendar sets switch which calendars you see. Event details offer a Join button for video calls and show other time zones. A weather forecast is optional and off until you turn it on.

On Apple Watch (TestFlight), glance at what is coming up, tick off a reminder or add one by voice or keyboard; your iPhone does the saving.

Stark keeps your events and to-dos in Apple Calendar and Reminders — Stark makes no network connection unless you turn on the optional weather forecast.

Plan your day

Stark can also plan your day. Open Plan from TODAY or TOMORROW in the agenda, drag open tasks onto a timeline as time blocks next to your events, resize a block to fit, and rework the plan as the day changes. Planning never changes a task's due date: a task you don't get to keeps its original date.

No accounts, no subscriptions, and no network connection unless you turn on the optional weather forecast.
