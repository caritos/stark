# Fantastical iPhone screenshot catalog

45 images, all viewed. Screens are 1290x2796 (iPhone Pro-class, iOS 26 "Liquid Glass" look). Coordinates below are rough fractions of the screen. Colours are described by eye, not sampled. "(guess)" marks inference.

Three capture sessions: Nov 15 2026 first launch (IMG_1862-1868, status bar 3:04-3:10 PM, the onboarding and permission flow; added later), Nov 16 2026 (IMG_1874-1895, status bar 1:27-1:29) and May 23 / Jun 9 2026 (IMG_8828-8840, IMG_9157-9159). The May/June set has far richer data (many events, tasks), so it is the best reference for dense views.

Global chrome seen on nearly every main screen:
- Large bold title top-left (about 34-40 pt, heavy weight, near-black). The year or the "2026" part is RED (system red) and the rest black.
- Top-right: a round white "glass" button with a black rocket icon (about 44 pt circle, soft shadow). On Tasks screens it widens into a pill holding rocket plus "..." (more).
- Bottom: a floating glass bar, three controls, no tab bar (details in Cross-screen patterns).
- Bottom-centre of the content: a grey pill/chevron "grabber" that toggles the expanded or collapsed area.

# Cross-screen patterns

**Navigation model**
- One screen, many "modes". The hamburger button (bottom-left, round glass) opens a popover with: Tasks / DayTicker / Calendar (screen family), then Day / Week / Month / Quarter / Year (calendar zoom levels), then Openings / Settings / Get Premium. The checkmark marks the current mode.
- No tab bar, no nav stack on the main screen. Settings, New Event, Openings and Edit are full-height sheets (radius about 40 pt) with a round glass X at left and a centred bold title; commit is a round filled BLUE check (Edit) or a pill "Add" (New Event). Pushed sub-screens use a round glass back chevron.
- Event detail is the only pushed screen with a labelled back pill ("< Sun, Nov 15") plus "..." and "Edit" glass buttons.
