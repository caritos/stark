# Natural-language quick add Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Typing a phrase in Add's Title offers one tap to fill date, time, repeat, kind and a clean title.

**Architecture:** A pure Kit parser (anchored rules driven by the `Calendar`, not `NSDataDetector`, which cannot take a fixed now) returns a suggestion; `AddItemView` shows it as a row and applies it on tap.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 / SwiftUI, Swift Testing, Go + static HTML for copy.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-quick-add-design.md`

## Review Focus
- "Call mom" (nothing recognised): no suggestion. Text that is only a date ("tomorrow"): no suggestion (empty title).
- "Lunch with Sarah at 1pm tomorrow": title "Lunch with Sarah", tomorrow 13:00, has time.
- "Pay rent every month": monthly repeat, title "Pay rent", no date needed.
- "Gym every Mon and Wed at 6am": weekly byDay Mon, Wed, 06:00 time.
- "Remind me to call the bank friday": kind reminder, title "call the bank".
- A date word inside a longer word (e.g. "Saturnalia") is not a date; "may" as a verb ("may I") must not become May: require the detector's match, and ignore a lone "may".
- Day boundaries and DST under the three zones.
