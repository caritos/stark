## Review Focus
- "Lunch tomorrow 1-2pm": start 13:00, end 14:00 (not 01:00 or 14:00 start); "Meeting 11-1pm": 11:00-13:00; "Call 3-4": no suggestion (no meridiem, no "at"); "Class 9am-10:30am"; "Flight 6pm-5pm": not a range; "Read pages 1-2pm"? treat as range only when a clock-time cue exists (am/pm or a colon time); "Chapter 3-4" is never a date.
- "next friday" on a Wednesday vs on a Friday vs on a Sunday; "this friday" on a Friday; DST week and the three zones.
- "Standup every weekday at 9am": weekly Mon-Fri at 09:00 starting on the next weekday date; "Trash every weekend": Sat+Sun, date-only start on the next matching day; "weekdays" alone (no "every") is not a repeat.
- Time range plus duration plus date words together ("Dinner friday 7-9pm for 2 hours": explicit range wins).
- After Apply the row is hidden for the same title; editing the title shows it again; Apply on a new phrase still works.
