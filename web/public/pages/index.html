STARK

Tasks and events, one agenda.

To Do List & Calendar for iOS

Stark is a minimal to-do list and calendar for iPhone. Reminders and events share one scrolling agenda, with a week, month and year calendar above it — no accounts, no cloud lock-in, no subscriptions.

Repeat anything daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, or with a custom rule. Search your whole history. Tag anything with +project, @context, %label or ~person, and mark whether you attended an event. Everything stays on your device — nothing is sent anywhere.

Plan your day

Stark can also plan your day. Open Plan from TODAY or TOMORROW in the agenda, drag open tasks onto a timeline as time blocks next to your events, resize a block to fit, and rework the plan as the day changes. Planning never changes a task's due date: a task you don't get to keeps its original date.

No accounts, no subscriptions, everything stays on your device.
